Welcome! I am Master of Science in Computer Science (MSCS) student at the University of Illinois Urbana Champaign, advised by Prof. Gagandeep Singh. Previously, I obtained my B.S. in Computer Science from Nanjing University, supervised by Prof. Linzhang Wang and Prof. Yu Wang at Software Engineering Group.

My research interests lie in the intersection of Formal Methods and Machine Learning. Currently I’m focusing on abstract-interpretation-based trustworthy AI systems.
