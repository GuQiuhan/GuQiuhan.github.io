Welcome! I am an incoming PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by [Prof. Gagandeep Singh](https://ggndpsngh.github.io/). I received my M.S. in Computer Science from UIUC under Prof. Gagandeep Singh's supervision as well in 2026. Before that, I obtained my B.S. in Computer Science from Nanjing University in 2024, supervised by [Prof. Linzhang Wang]() and [Prof. Yu Wang](https://itwoi.github.io/) at [Software Engineering Group](https://seg.nju.edu.cn/index.action). 

My research interests lie in the intersection of Formal Methods and Machine Learning. Currently I'm focusing on Abstract Interpretation for Artificial Intelligence (AI^2). I'm also broadly interested in Neural Network Verification, Probabilistic Programming, and Differential Privacy.
